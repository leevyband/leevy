“There is rhythm in the sadness; there is rhythm in the sod”

Leevy are a five-piece folk-punk band from the boggy depths of rural Ireland.

From the smallest pubs to the biggest festivals, the band have built a reputation for mesmerising live performances that are as romantic as they are chaotic.

Their sound can roar with pounding drums, banjos, whistles and heavy guitars, or sway with delicate folk instrumentation and whispered harmonies, rooted in the traditions and culture of their home.

RTÉ Folk Award nominees, Leevy released their debut album, Baile Mhúirne, named after their Irish-speaking hometown, in May 2026. The record debuted at No. 2 in the Irish Independent Album Charts, establishing the band as one of the most exciting emerging acts in Irish folk.

Described as a marriage of The Pogues and Pixies, Leevy fuses raucous energy with a distinctive songwriting voice and a deep sense of place and self. Through the lyrics of frontman Amhlaoibh McSweeney, their songs speak of the tragedy, humour and hope in the lives of ordinary people everywhere.
